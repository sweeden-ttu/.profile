# Scott Weeden

Graduate researcher at Texas Tech University (Master of Computer Applications). I work on **verifiable AI agents**: I classify what an agent can do by where its behavior falls in the Chomsky hierarchy, and I check its traces against formal languages.

**Currently**
- 🦴 **RSNA Knee Abnormality Detection** (Kaggle): a Keras pipeline with dataset profiling, view/orientation analysis, and a modern pretrained backbone
- 🤖 **Google Gemma 4 Developer Agent** (Kaggle): agentic workflows on Gemma 4
- 📄 *A Trace Language Theory of Agents*, under review at AAAI
